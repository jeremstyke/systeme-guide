Systeme.io vs Kajabi

Prices checked on the official websites in November 2026. They may change.

Kajabi is a well-known premium platform for course creators and coaches. Systeme.io covers the same core needs (courses, funnels, emails, automations, website), but with a free plan and much lower prices. Here is a detailed comparison based on the official pricing pages.

The 30-second summary

  • Entry price: $0 at Systeme.io, $179 per month at Kajabi ($143 billed yearly).
  • Free plan: yes at Systeme.io, no at Kajabi (14-day trial and 30-day money-back guarantee).
  • Fees: 0% at Systeme.io; at Kajabi, card fees from 2.9% + 30¢ (US) with Kajabi Payments, or extra fees if you use another processor.
  • Our pick for most people: Systeme.io.

What it costs over a year

Kajabi's Basic plan costs at least $1,716 per year for 5 products and 2,500 contacts. With Systeme.io, the $17 Startup plan gives you 5 courses and 5,000 contacts: twice the contacts for a fraction of the price. And Systeme.io's Unlimited plan ($97 per month, everything unlimited) is still cheaper than Kajabi's lowest plan.

Fees on your sales

This is a key difference. Systeme.io takes 0%: you only pay Stripe's or PayPal's usual fees. Kajabi takes no revenue share, but its card fees through Kajabi Payments range from 2.9% + 30¢ (Basic) to 2.7% + 30¢ (Pro) in the US, and if you use another payment processor Kajabi adds 2% (Basic), 1% (Growth) or 0.5% (Pro).

  • A free plan forever to launch your first course without paying anything.

Ease of use

Both tools are built for non-technical people. Systeme.io bets on simplicity: one dashboard, templates and a drag-and-drop editor. Kajabi offers a very polished experience, but its price makes it mainly a tool for established creators.

Courses and community

This is Kajabi's home turf, which counts its limits in "products": 5 on Basic, 50 on Growth, unlimited on Pro. Systeme.io counts courses: 1 on Free, 5 on Startup, 20 on Webinar, unlimited on Unlimited. Both offer a members area and a community for your students, and let you sell with a one-time payment, a subscription or a payment plan.

Funnels and email

Systeme.io started as a sales funnel tool: order bumps, one-click upsells, A/B tests, coupon codes and deadline funnels are at its core. For email, sends are unlimited on every Systeme.io plan. Kajabi also includes email and sales pipelines, but counts its limits in contacts, with extra contacts at $125 per month per block of 25,000.

Team

If you work with many collaborators, Kajabi has an edge: 2 admins on Basic, 11 on Growth and 26 on Pro. Systeme.io offers assistant accounts, enough for a solo creator or a small team.

Payments and migration

Systeme.io connects to Stripe, Apple Pay, PayPal, Razorpay, Flutterwave, Mercado Pago, Xendit and cash on delivery, with no percentage taken. If you are already on Kajabi, Systeme.io advertises free migration.

Which one should you choose?

Choose Systeme.io if you are launching your first course, want to keep costs low, or have a small or medium audience. You get courses, funnels, emails and automations for a fraction of the price.

Choose Kajabi if you already run a large business, have a comfortable budget and need many admins.

Verdict

Kajabi is a good platform, but at $179 per month minimum and with no free plan, it is mainly for established businesses. Systeme.io covers the same basics from $0, takes no percentage of your sales and stays cheaper even on its unlimited plan. For most course creators, Systeme.io is the better choice.
